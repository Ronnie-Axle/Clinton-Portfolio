KICKBOARD DRILLS

Focuses on lower body conditioning, kick alignment, and leg endurance. Builds fundamental kicking power and strengthens the core foundation required for all competitive strokes.

Builds technique, kicking power, and core endurance.

FREESTYLE (FRONT CRAWL)

Master high-elbow catch, continuous rhythmic kicking, and efficient bilateral breathing. Designed for maximum speed and hydrodynamic efficiency in the water.

Improves speed, strength, and cardiovascular stamina.

BREASTSTROKE

Develop synchronized arm pulls and frog kicks with proper glide timing. Excellent for building upper body control, leg coordination, and breath control.

Builds control, upper body strength, and balance.

BACKSTROKE

Emphasizes relaxed supine body position, full shoulder rotation, and continuous flutter kicking. Ideal for improving posture, shoulder mobility, and spinal alignment.

Improves posture, spine alignment, and flexibility.
Why Swimming Is Good For You

HEALTH BENEFITS

SWIPE OR SCROLL HORIZONTALLY →

Benefit 01

Improves Heart Health

Strengthens your cardiovascular system, lowers blood pressure, and dramatically improves systemic circulation through low-impact aerobic exercise.

Benefit 02

Builds Lung Capacity

Controlled breathing techniques train your lungs to process oxygen efficiently, expanding pulmonary capacity and breathing stamina.

Benefit 03

Tones Your Muscles

Water provides 12x the resistance of air. Works every major muscle group simultaneously to build lean, balanced functional strength.

Benefit 04

Weight Management

Burns significant calories while keeping metabolic rate elevated. An ideal high-calorie burning activity for long-term health and fitness.

Benefit 05

Reduces Stress

The rhythmic nature of aquatic movement releases endorphins, reduces anxiety, boosts mental clarity, and relaxes mind and body.

Benefit 06

Flexibility & Joint Health

Zero impact on joints. Water buoyancy relieves pressure on bones while active strokes expand joint range of motion and mobility.

Benefit 07

Builds Confidence

Overcoming fear of deep water builds resilience, personal discipline, focus, and essential life-saving aquatic confidence.
